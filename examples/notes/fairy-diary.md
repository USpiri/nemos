# A Letter Most Urgent, from the Hollow of Silverwick

**To:** Aldric Thornstep, Wanderer of the Old Paths
**From:** Syllara Dewveil, Keeper of the Gossamer Records, Third Ring
**Date:** 14th Petal of Verdantine, Year 412 of the Long Twilight

---

Dear brave and wonderfully clumsy adventurer,

I have been watching you from the bracken since you crossed the Mosswhisper River — three times, from three different directions, each more confident than the last — and I must say, your sense of direction is *absolutely* charming for someone who has managed to be lost in the same meadow twice before breakfast. I took notes. They are very entertaining.

But I write now with urgency rather than amusement, for something stirs in the Thornwood that has not stirred in many hundreds of years. The old roots speak of it in their slow, grinding tongue. Even the moths, who know far more than they ever say, have changed their flight paths — and moths do not change their paths lightly. It is, as my grandmother used to whisper into the foxglove: *a matter of some concern.*

> ✦ the eastern edge of the Thornwood, at the hour of the pale moon ✦

The place you seek is not marked on any human map I have seen — and I have read most of them, while their owners slept and their candles burned low. The entrance to the **Archive of Woven Names** lies where the river forks at the twin standing stones near the village of Craventhal. From the right angle, at the right hour, the stones look exactly like a heron caught mid-leap. You cannot see them at all unless you blink precisely as the sun touches the water's surface. I know this sounds unreasonable. Most true things do.

Beware the Tangle Guard who patrols the third path past the elder grove. She is not unkind — she once helped a lost shepherd find his flock, and only kept three of the sheep for herself, which is considered quite generous by Tangle Guard standards — but she carries a memory spanning four hundred years and has *absolutely no patience* for those who forget to knock before entering her territory. Bring something sweet. She has a well-known weakness for candied violets. The dark purple ones from Caervelm, mind you, not the pale imitations they sell in Duskgate market. She will know the difference, and you will regret it if you try.

Take note also of the creatures you meet along the way, for they speak truths that no map ever could. The three-legged fox near Grimvale knows every shortcut through the Fenmire but should not be fed dried plums, whatever she tells you about her dietary requirements. The grey heron at the mirror pool charges a toll of one secret per crossing. It need not be an important secret. He simply collects them the way your kind collects coins — without plan or purpose, but with extraordinary dedication. Give him something small. Something true. He can always tell the difference.

> ✦ the twin standing stones at the river fork, seen from the northern bank ✦

I have enclosed with this letter one vial of true-seeing mist — the *small* one, sealed in blue wax; do **not** open the large one, I have genuinely forgotten what I put in it and the results last time were regrettable and also slightly luminous — and three seeds from the Whispering Fig, which you should plant somewhere you feel welcome, because if you do not, they will simply follow you. You have been warned. It happened to a cartographer in Year 389 and he was finding seedlings in his boots for a decade.

One final thing: the Archive does not open for weapons, crowns, or those who announce their own importance before they knock. It opens for *questions*. Bring a good one. The kind you are actually afraid to hear answered.

Return this letter to the copper beech hollow at the edge of the Verdantine Wood when you have read it. There is writing on the other side that I have not yet finished, and I would very much prefer not to lose my place.

---

Travel well, watch your step on the root-paths, and please try not to fall into any more rivers —

*Syllara Dewveil*
*Keeper of the Gossamer Records, Third Ring*
*Hollow of Silverwick — Verdantine Wood*

✦
